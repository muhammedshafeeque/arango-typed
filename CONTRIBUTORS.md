## How to Contribute

We welcome contributions from the community! Here's how you can help:

1. **Report Bugs**: Found a bug? Please [open an issue](https://github.com/muhammedshafeeque/arango-typed/issues) with details.
2. **Suggest Features**: Have an idea? We'd love to hear it! [Open a feature request](https://github.com/muhammedshafeeque/arango-typed/issues).
3. **Submit Pull Requests**: See [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md) for guidelines.
4. **Improve Documentation**: Documentation improvements are always welcome!
5. **Share Feedback**: Your feedback helps us improve!
